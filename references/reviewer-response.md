# Reviewer Response

## Response Strategy

Classify each comment:

- accept and revise;
- accept in principle but revise differently;
- clarify without major text change;
- respectfully disagree with evidence;
- request/journal-format task;
- unclear comment requiring interpretation.

## Revision Authority And Linked Changes

Keep the reviewer's request, the author's chosen response, and authorized edits
distinct. A deferred or declined item does not become a prerequisite for another
approved change. Explain a material unresolved problem within scope without
silently reinstating work the author excluded.

For contribution repositioning, identify the claims withdrawn, content retained,
and dependent locations requiring synchronization. Apply the linked-revision
check in `structure-and-prose.md`; a changed concept label does not authorize
rewriting all related content. Keep a local comparator-name or language fix
local unless broader revision is authorized. Do not duplicate external roadmap
or project-memory workflows here.

## Medical Content Rules

- Answer the reviewer point, not a more convenient adjacent issue.
- Preserve clinical and evidence boundaries.
- Do not overpromise new claims, analyses, or recommendations.
- If disagreeing, use source-based reasoning and calm language.
- Ensure manuscript revision and response letter agree.

## Output

For each reviewer point, provide:

- interpretation of the request;
- risk if mishandled;
- recommended response posture;
- manuscript change target;
- draft response text when asked.
